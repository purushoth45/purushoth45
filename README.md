## About me

I design and build native Apple ecosystem products with a strong focus on user experience, maintainability, and performance. My work blends product thinking with clean engineering practices to deliver apps that feel natural, responsive, and polished.

- 🔭 Building modern iOS experiences with Swift, SwiftUI, and architecture-first thinking.
- 🌱 Exploring Apple platform innovation through ARKit, CoreML, Swift concurrency, and design systems.
- 🧠 Focused on building scalable, testable, production-ready apps that are a joy to use.
- 💬 Open to collaboration, product partnerships, mobile engineering opportunities, and open-source contributions.
